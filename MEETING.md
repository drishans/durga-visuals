# Visual direction meeting

Open **outputs/demo.html** in a browser. It works offline. Use the arrow buttons or left/right keys; motion samples repeat silently. For the Mac, copy and extract **outputs/meeting-demo.zip**, then open `demo.html` inside the extracted folder. This is a look-and-feel review, not a rehearsal of the final screen setup.

For a quick start, play [the 90-second silent overview](outputs/meeting-showreel.mp4). Use [the look sheet](outputs/previews/meeting-collection.jpg) to compare the 12 still directions, then open the demo to revisit favorites. Both are also included in the ZIP.

## A simple 10-minute conversation

1. Show the motion opener, then compare the performance, dance/DJ, and daytime options. Skip freely; there is no need to present every variation.
2. Choose two or three visual families that feel right for this year's golden-glamour theme.
3. Match the favorites to event moments. Keep performances calmer, use stronger looks during dancing, and confirm where the existing Durga/Sindoor imagery belongs.
4. Agree who will cue visual changes and which must-have artwork, sponsor material, or announcements need space.
5. Describe the camera idea as an optional interactive moment. Its feed and effects still need testing; it is not required for the main show to work.

Useful reactions: too bright/dark, too busy/quiet, too formal/playful, stronger gold versus accent colors. Ask about the mood and timing rather than shader settings.

## Questions for the panel supplier, after the meeting

- What computer output resolution and refresh rate should we send? Where does the 1248 x 540 image sit in that signal, and who handles the mapping/scaling?
- What connection and adapter should the Mac use, and can we test that exact chain before the event?
- Who controls wall brightness, and when can we check a bright daytime image and a dark nighttime image on the actual wall?

## Questions for the photographer

- Can we receive a spare clean crowd-camera feed while you keep your normal shooting duties? What output format and capture adapter were used previously?
- Where can the camera and Mac sit, how long is the cable route, and who controls framing during the interactive moment?

No contact details are needed to prepare the demo. Confirm hardware with the relevant person before buying anything.

The complete technical rehearsal and camera sequence remains in [SHOW-PLAN.md](SHOW-PLAN.md). The existing collection and live shader preview remain separate from this small offline presentation.
