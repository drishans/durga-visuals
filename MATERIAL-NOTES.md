# Material notes

All show media is now on the Mac in ignored `show-media/`. USB dGold is the untouched backup. See [MAC-HANDOFF.md](MAC-HANDOFF.md) for the folder table.

## Gold Sparkles playlist

[Sanch's playlist: Gold Sparkles](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLTMq9yn_Rskq8ueXMWPc08YW8Wu_gB1ZX) is the primary style reference. All 14 clips were downloaded with yt-dlp (video only, up to 1080p; clip 05 is the first 5 minutes of a 1-hour video) and sorted after frame review:

- `DJ/`: 01 dust burst, 03 golden stage (faint stock watermark), 10 glowing pathway, 11 gold fibers sphere on blue, 12 gold dust overlay on black (use Screen/Add), 14 diagonal light streaks.
- `Dandiya/`: 02 stars on navy, 04 stars on red (10 min), 05 spotlight dust, 06 light rays, 07 bokeh flare, 08 dense bokeh, 09 lens flare particles, 13 glitter close-up.

Most are slow and elegant; beat energy has to come from Avenue BPM effects, shaders and title cues. 05, 06 and 09 also suit daytime backing.

## LED DURGA folder (from dGold)

- `DJ/LED DJ Visuals/`: neon tunnels, kaleidoscopes, `trippy.mov` and the `sunset*` 4K clips. Despite "4K" in many names, most are 720p/1080p H.264. Review loop joins before relying on them. Several files are duplicates (`(1)` copies).
- `Dandiya/LED Dandiya Visuals/`: mandalas, kaleidoscopes, flower and LED tunnels.
- `Daytime/`: Durga Puja films, Sindur Khela/Bijoya, genai images. The old Bijoya video has dated branding; it is a reference, not an approved slide. `Sundur Khela Video 1.mp4` is empty on dGold and was not kept.

## Other

Prior-year 2024 Europe and 2025 Royal material was skipped (wrong theme). The [Resolume footage catalog](https://resolume.com/footage) is another source of VJ packs; check the license before buying. When a Ma portrait is used, keep it stable with only a faint base/edge accent. Strong kaleidoscope, zoom and color changes belong in DJ/Dandiya scenes.
